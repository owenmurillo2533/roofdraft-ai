The Problem

Most roofing contractors do not lose jobs because they cannot do the work.

They lose jobs because the follow-up is late, the proposal looks rushed, or the homeowner never gets a clear next step. Homeowners compare professionalism, not just price, and a rough estimate can make a solid company look disorganized.

RoofDraftAI is for the contractor who wants better customer-facing documents, faster follow-up, and a lighter workflow than a full CRM just to send a stronger proposal.

Writing proposals from scratch takes time after inspections and sales calls.
Sales reps can send inconsistent messages that weaken the company image.
Review and referral requests are easy to skip after the job is finished.
Large CRMs can feel heavy if you mainly need cleaner customer documents.
What the homeowner sees
The goal is not flashy wording. It is faster follow-up, cleaner presentation, and a proposal that feels ready to approve.
